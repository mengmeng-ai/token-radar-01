How the radar scores a protocol

Every row is a protocol whose DefiLlama record carries no token (no market cap, no CoinGecko id, no symbol). The board covers all 40 protocols on the snapshot's watchlist, plus the strongest pre-token protocols below the $50M floor, by TVL.

  • TVL, log-compressed: TVL^0.9 × 0.25 — size matters, but a giant does not swallow the board.
  • Category weight: Dexs 1.15 · Lending 1.12 · Derivatives 1.10 · Yield 1.06 · Yield Aggregator 1.06 · RWA 1.05 · Onchain Capital Allocator 1.04 · Risk Curators 1.03 · other 1.00 · Bridge / Liquid Staking / Restaking 0.85.
  • Growth: 1 + clamp(7-day TVL change, −50%, +150%) ÷ 100 × 0.6 — a hot week lifts, a cold week cuts.
  • Maturity: 0.70 + min(age, 1460 days) ÷ 1460 × 0.50 — the longer a protocol runs with no token recorded, the more pent-up launch value. Unknown age counts as 365 days.

Protocols whose token already trades are kept apart in the Live tokens tab and shown at their actual market cap — the market's own value, not a model's. A farm plan is generated per protocol from its category, chains, TVL and age, with what each step costs and how long it takes. An implied value is this entry's own claim: it does not predict whether or when a token is issued.
